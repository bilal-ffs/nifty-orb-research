# Contributing

Run `python scripts/run_tests.py` and `python scripts/check_publication.py`.
Keep signal, fill, feature and outcome timing explicit. Changes to research rules
need a separately named experiment and must not silently rewrite historical
claims. Keep OOS observations out of tuning and report negative findings.

Do not submit private data, model pickles, broker credentials or third-party
source without permission. Reports must identify units, costs, sample period,
selection history and unresolved assumptions. See docs/REPRODUCING.md before
running commands that overwrite local research outputs.
